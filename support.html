Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save a link?

Open Safari (or any app), tap the Share button, scroll down and select "Save to LinkVault". The link will be automatically saved with title and description.

I don't see "Save to LinkVault" in the share menu

Scroll to the bottom of the share sheet and tap "Edit Actions" or "More". Find LinkVault and enable it. You can also drag it to the top for easy access.

How do I organize my links?

Tap any link to open the detail view. You can then add categories, tags, and notes. Tap "Done" to save your changes.

Where are my links stored?

All your links are stored locally on your device. They never leave your iPhone and are not synced to any cloud service.

Can I export my links?

Currently, LinkVault stores all data locally on your device. Export functionality may be added in a future update.

How do I delete a link?

Swipe left on any link and tap the red Delete button. You can also archive links to keep them but remove them from your inbox.

What's the badge number on the app icon?

The badge shows how many unprocessed links are in your inbox. The number decreases when you organize or archive links.

How do I search for links?

Tap the Search tab and enter keywords. LinkVault searches through titles, descriptions, and URLs to find your links.

The app isn't loading metadata for my link

Some websites block automatic metadata extraction. You can manually edit the title and description in the link detail view.
